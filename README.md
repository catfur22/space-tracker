# space-tracker
I wanted to make something for crossroads that didn't require hardware of 3d design and so I decided to make my own satelite tracker because I have a plane tracker app on my phone that is pretty cool and I wanted something like that. I also wanted a project what didn't take much time but I had issues with the map. I got it to work though and I am very proud of it. I also wanted a simple project to do. This sattelite tracker tracks position in latitude and longitude and it also tracks velocity and altitude using public sattelite data sites. It also tries to show you what type of sattelite it is and it has each one a different color bc why not. I have seen gps sattelites and navigation and telecom ones among more. It also shows you where it has been and its pretty cool. It also updates around once every 30 seconds.
